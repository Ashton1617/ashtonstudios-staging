STRAIGHT answers.

what if i don't like it?

then you don't pay. the preview is free and there's no obligation. if you go ahead and the finished ad isn't right, you get one round of changes included.

what about music?

i deliver the ad without music, so you can add a track from instagram's, facebook's or tiktok's own licensed libraries when you post. that's the version that's allowed to run on their platforms. or you take it with the voiceover and no music at all. either way, nothing in it can get your post taken down for a copyright claim.

who owns it?

you do. once it's paid for, the files are yours to post anywhere, for as long as you like, with no licence fee and no credit required.

how fast?

48 hours from your photos, clips and logo landing with me. the preview before that is quicker still, because it's cut from what's already on your website.

no. previews made for a specific business are private links, not shown here, and are taken down on request or after 60 days. the only real business on this page is a client who agreed to be here.
