coaching

Built around you, not a timetable.

Every session is planned for the swimmer in front of him. Here is what that looks like.

One-to-one sessions

In a main pool for starts, turns and confidence, or an endless pool for filming your stroke and holding a set pace. Technique, then strength, then better training patterns.

from £36 · 30 minutes

Two lanes at St Albans School. Ross recommends ten swimmers, five a lane, so everyone gets coached and everyone has someone to chase.

about ross

Twenty years in the water, fifteen on the side of it.

Ross Clewlow has been a competitive swimmer for over twenty years, a swimming teacher for as long, and a coach for fifteen. He holds a degree in sport science and coaching, and set up Train With Ross in 2016 to coach the way he thinks it should be done: one swimmer at a time.

He has coached a former Paralympic champion who still trains with him for the challenge of it, and taken a swimmer from no front crawl to a Dover–Calais Channel relay in four years.

"Train hard and push boundaries — but most of all, enjoy training."
Ross
