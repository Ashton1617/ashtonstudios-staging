Services

From a single socket to a full three-phase fit-out.

Every job is carried out to the current 18th Edition regulations and finished with the right certificate. Here is what people call us for most.

Rewires

Full or partial, planned room by room so you can keep living in the house. Boards lifted and put back, chases made good, dust kept to a minimum.

01

Fault finding

The thing we are known for. Intermittent trips, dead circuits, warm sockets, flickering lights: found and fixed, not guessed at.

02

Fuse box & consumer units

Old fuse boards replaced with modern RCBO consumer units, surge protection where it makes sense, and a certificate for your records.

EICRs & landlord certificates

Electrical condition reports for homeowners, landlords and businesses, with plain-English findings and a fixed price to put anything right.

03

Fire alarms & emergency lighting

Design, installation and periodic testing for offices, shops, workshops and HMOs, including wire-free systems that leave no mess.

04

Sockets, lighting & heating

Extra sockets and USB points, new lighting inside and out, security lights, cooker circuits and night storage heating repairs.

05

Three-phase, industrial & machinery

Distribution board upgrades, three-phase supplies, steel-wire-armoured runs, machine repairs and periodic testing for factories, farms and workshops. Speedy response on breakdowns, because a stopped line costs more every hour it stays stopped.
